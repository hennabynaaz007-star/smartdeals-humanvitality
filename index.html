✂️ Tailoring & Designer Dress Studio

Measurements · Cutting · Stitching
✨ ORIGINAL TAILORING GUIDE

Measure smart. Cut neatly. Stitch beautifully.

Practical garment measurements, cutting notes and stitching checkpoints in one colorful guide.

📏
MEASURE
WITH CARE
👗

Kurti / Kameez

Measure: full length, shoulder, bust, waist, hip, armhole, sleeve length and neck depth.

Cut: mark the centre line first, then shoulder, armhole and side shaping; add seam allowance consistently.

Stitch: join shoulder → finish neckline → attach sleeves → close sides → hem.

🌸

Anarkali / Gown

Measure: bodice length, bust, waist, shoulder, armhole, sleeve and desired flare/length.

Cut: divide the flare evenly across panels; mark grainline and matching points before cutting.

Stitch: assemble bodice → join panels → attach sleeves → finish neckline → hem.

👚

Blouse

Measure: bust, under-bust, shoulder, armhole, blouse length, front/back neck and sleeve.

Cut: draft front/back separately; mark darts/princess shaping and keep matching notches.

Stitch: darts/panels → shoulders → neckline finish → sleeves → side seams → hooks/zip.

🩳

Salwar / Churidar

Measure: waist, hip, rise, full length, thigh, calf/ankle and desired bottom opening.

Cut: use the measured rise and leg width; keep sufficient seam allowance and grain direction.

Stitch: join leg seams → crotch/rise → waistband → bottom finish.

💃

Sharara / Gharara

Measure: waist, hip, rise, length and flare; decide panel width according to the design.

Cut: divide flare into symmetrical panels and label every panel before cutting.

Stitch: join panels carefully → attach waistband → finish hems and decorative layers.

🧒

Kids Wear

Measure: chest, shoulder, armhole, waist, length and sleeve; allow comfortable movement.

Cut: keep simple shapes, clearly mark front/back and use safe seam finishes.

Stitch: assemble main body → neckline → sleeves → side seams → hem.

📐 Measurement Checklist

Shoulder Bust / Chest Waist Hip Armhole Sleeve Length Dress Length Neck Depth Rise Thigh Calf / Bottom Desired Ease

Tip: Take body measurements snugly without pulling the tape. Add garment ease and seam allowance according to the fabric, design and fit you want.

01 Measure Record body measurements
02 Draft Mark the pattern clearly
03 Cut Check grain & matching points
04 Stitch Follow the assembly order
05 Finish Press, hem & inspect

📌 These are educational drafting/cutting guidelines. Exact formulas vary by garment style, fabric, fitting preference and tailor's method. Always make a test piece for a new design.
