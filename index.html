✂️ Tailoring Studio

Cutting • Stitching • Perfect Fit

Learn practical tailoring basics, body measurements, cutting ideas and stitching inspiration for beautiful everyday and occasion wear.

🧵 From Measurement to Beautiful Fit

A good outfit begins with accurate measurements and careful cutting. Whether you are stitching a simple Kurti, elegant Anarkali, designer blouse or Sharara, understanding the basic measurements makes the final fitting much easier.

✓
Measure Carefully
Keep the measuring tape comfortable, not too tight.
✓
Mark Before Cutting
Double-check important measurements before cutting fabric.
✓
Leave Seam Allowance
Keep suitable extra fabric wherever adjustments may be needed.

📐 Quick Measurement Checklist

Measurements vary from person to person. Use your own body measurements instead of copying a standard size blindly.

01
Bust / Chest
Measure around the fullest area.
02
Waist
Measure around your natural waistline.
03
Hip
Measure around the fullest part.
04
Length
Measure from the required starting point to desired length.

👗 Dress Cutting & Stitching Gallery

Different styles need different cutting and fitting approaches.

🌸 Kurti / Kameez

A versatile style for everyday wear. Focus on shoulder, bust, waist, armhole and length measurements.

👑 Anarkali / Gown

Flared silhouettes need careful length, waist placement and flare planning for a graceful fall.

✨ Designer Blouse

Blouse fitting depends strongly on bust, under-bust, shoulder, neckline and armhole measurements.

💫 Sharara / Gharara

Wide-leg designs require thoughtful waist, hip, rise, length and flare measurements.

📏 Different Body-Type Measurement Examples

These are examples only. Always take fresh measurements for the actual garment.

🌷

Balanced / Straight Fit

  • Shoulder: measure shoulder bone to shoulder bone.
  • Bust: measure around fullest area.
  • Waist: measure natural waist.
  • Hip: measure fullest area.
  • Keep garment ease according to fabric and style.
🌸

Curved / Hip-Focused Fit

  • Take bust, waist and hip separately.
  • Do not assume bust and hip are the same proportion.
  • Check hip ease before cutting fitted garments.
  • For flared dresses, mark the waistline accurately.
  • Check side seam alignment.
🌺

Fuller / Comfort Fit

  • Take measurements without pulling the tape tightly.
  • Consider comfortable wearing ease.
  • Check armhole comfort carefully.
  • Plan neckline and length according to design.
  • Keep adjustment allowance where useful.

📐 Measurement Planning Table

Use this as a checklist while taking your own measurements.

Measurement How to Measure Used For
Shoulder Across the shoulder points Kurti, Kameez, Blouse
Bust / Chest Around fullest area All upper garments
Waist Around natural waist Fitted & waist-based designs
Hip Around fullest hip area Long Kurti, Gown, Sharara
Armhole Around the armhole area Sleeve fitting
Sleeve Length Shoulder point to desired sleeve end Short, elbow & full sleeves
Garment Length Starting point to desired hem Kurti, Gown, Dresses

🧷 How to Take Measurements

Simple steps for more consistent measurements.

1

Stand Naturally

Stand straight in a comfortable natural posture. Do not hold your breath or pull the stomach in.

2

Keep the Tape Level

Keep the measuring tape parallel to the floor for bust, waist and hip measurements.

3

Don't Pull Too Tight

The tape should touch the body comfortably without pressing into the skin.

4

Write Measurements Immediately

Record each measurement clearly with the unit used, such as inches or centimetres.

5

Measure Twice

Recheck important measurements before cutting expensive or limited fabric.

6

Make a Trial Where Possible

For a new pattern or difficult fabric, a trial fitting can help identify adjustments before final stitching.

🧵 Smart Tailoring Tips

Small details can make cutting and stitching easier.

✂️ Mark Before Cutting

Clearly mark centre lines, seam lines, neckline, darts and important reference points before cutting.

📏 Check Grain & Direction

Pay attention to fabric grain, print direction, embroidery and nap before placing the pattern.

🪡 Test the Stitch

On unfamiliar fabric, test the stitch length and tension on a small scrap before starting the garment.

👗 Consider Ease

A garment measurement is not always the same as the body's measurement. Wearing ease depends on the style.

🔄 Keep Adjustment Space

When appropriate, leave useful seam allowance so small fitting adjustments can be made later.

🧼 Finish Neatly

Press seams, trim loose threads and finish raw edges according to the fabric and garment design.
